01

First, find the layer

"Restricted" is five different things wearing the same word. Your personal profile, your Business Manager, your ad account, your Facebook page, and your Instagram are separate assets, and Meta restricts them separately.

Three places tell you which one it is:

  • Account Quality lists restrictions on every layer in one place. Start here.
  • Your Facebook page's Boost button. If you can click it, the page is not restricted. If it is greyed out, the page is, and "See details" tells you why. (Jen)
  • Business Settings shows whether the Business Manager itself is disabled.
A rejected ad is not a restriction

If Account Quality shows nothing restricted and what you actually have is one or more rejected ads, close this page and open Door 03. Different problem, much smaller fix.

02

Which layer, what still works, what to do

This is Jen's troubleshooting, laid out by layer. (Jen)

LayerHow you knowWhat still worksWhat to do
Personal profile restricted from advertising Account Quality shows it on the profile. You cannot create a new ad account, Business Manager, or page. Nothing, from this profile. Create a new profile. New email address. Same name and date of birth, exactly, in case you are asked to confirm identity. A brand-new selfie that has never been posted anywhere. No phone number needed. Then use it like a real person for 30 minutes a day. This is the only situation that needs a new profile.
Business Manager disabled Business Settings says disabled. Any page attached to it says it cannot run ads. Your Instagram (disconnect it and keep it). Your domain. Your payment method. In this order: chat with Facebook help if the option appears; request review through Business Manager; check Account Quality. If no restriction shows there, create a new Business Manager. You are allowed two per profile. Then Door 05 to rebuild.
Ad account restricted Account Quality lists the ad account. Everything else on the Business Manager. But you can never create another ad account on this Business Manager again. Switch to the spare ad account you created earlier. If you have none, it is a new Business Manager (Door 05). Season the fresh account with engagement ads before anything else, and leave whatever is still running elsewhere alone. (Keala)
Facebook page restricted Boost button greyed out. Your profile, your Business Manager, your ad account, your Instagram. Click "See details" and request review. If your profile is healthy, you can simply create a new page and connect it to your existing Business Manager. No new Business Manager needed. A page stuck on a disabled Business Manager can be moved to a new one and it will run ads again.
Instagram tied to a restricted page or Business Manager Instagram itself is fine; it is the connection that is broken. All of Instagram. Instagram, then Edit profile. Disconnect the restricted page. Connect the Instagram account to your healthy Business Manager. Your Instagram and Facebook page do not need to be connected to each other at all.
03

Wait. I thought we never request review?

Right, and the two rules live together. The no-review rule is for a single rejected ad on a healthy account. Requesting review there invites a human to look at an account that is otherwise fine, and that human can take action on the whole thing. Not worth it for one ad.

Once a Business Manager, page, or ad account is already restricted, that damage is done. The review is the one door Meta offers, so Jen's order (chat, then review, then Account Quality) is the right sequence here.

But do not wait on it

Start building the spare in parallel the same day. Reviews can take weeks and often come back with nothing. The rebuild is the plan; the review is a lottery ticket you buy on the way.

04

The rebuild, in one paragraph

You do not need a new Facebook profile (unless the profile itself is restricted), a new payment method, a new domain, or a new page (unless the page is restricted). You do need a new email address for the new Business Manager, a new business account name, to move your domain, page, and Instagram out of the old Business Manager, a new ad account, a new pixel attached to it, your domain re-verified, and a simple engagement campaign as the very first thing that runs. (Jen)

Step by step, with the checklist, on Door 05.

05

While you rebuild

Leave any ads that are still running, anywhere, completely alone. Ads that were live before the trouble tend to stay fine as long as nobody touches them. (Keala)

Get engagement ads running on the new account the same day it exists. A brand-new account with a clean approval history behind it is worth far more than a brand-new account that has never been tested. Door 02 has the settings.

This is why we never run ads from a personal profile or straight from Instagram

Run from your personal profile's business manager and a restriction can take the profile. Run from Instagram's Boost button and a restriction can take your Instagram. Run from a Business Manager ad account and the worst case is a rebuild, because there is always a way to create a new one. (Jen)
