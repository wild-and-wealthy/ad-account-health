Setting up or rebuilding Business Manager

01

Why the structure matters

Every rule on this site rests on one decision: run your ads from a Business Manager ad account, not from your personal profile and not from the Instagram app.

The reason is blast radius. If your personal profile's business manager gets restricted, you risk the profile. If Instagram's Boost button gets you restricted, you risk the Instagram account. A Business Manager ad account can be replaced. Your face and your following cannot.

A few structural facts to hold onto: you can have two Business Managers per profile. One Facebook page can be connected to only one Business Manager at a time, so a spare Business Manager must not be linked to your page until you need it. And once an ad account on a Business Manager is restricted, you can never create another ad account on that Business Manager.

02

Clean setup checklist

Use this for a first-time setup or a rebuild after a restriction. Same steps either way.

  • A new email address to create the Business Manager with.
  • A new business account name.
  • Business Settings, then Accounts: add your Facebook page, create your ad account, add your Instagram account.
  • If this is a rebuild: move your domain, page, and Instagram out of the old Business Manager first.
  • Business Settings, then Data sources: create a pixel, rename it, and use "Email instructions" to send the install steps to whoever owns your funnel.
  • Verify your domain in the new Business Manager.
  • Verify the business. Business Settings, then Security Center. Legal business name typed exactly as it appears on your documents, no abbreviations. EIN, address, phone, and one supporting document (EIN letter, license, articles, or a bank statement).
  • Check whether your Events Manager still asks you to configure web events (this used to be called Aggregated Events Measurement). If it does, set up the two steps for a two-step funnel. Meta has been phasing this out, so do not worry if the option is not there.
  • Make the very first campaign a simple engagement or like campaign. Nothing else runs until that has approved. Door 02 has the settings.
Two things that look like verification and are not

"Verify yourself or an organization" is for elections and politics. Skip it. Meta Verified is the paid blue badge. It does nothing for ad approvals. The one you want is Business Verification inside Security Center, and it is free.

You need, you do not need

You need
A new email address. A new business account name. A new ad account. A new pixel attached to it. Your domain re-verified. Business verification. An engagement campaign first.
You do not need
A new Facebook profile (unless the profile itself is restricted). A new payment method. A new domain. A new Facebook page (unless the page is restricted).
03

Spare ad accounts

Business Settings, then Business Info, shows your ad account creation limit. A brand-new Business Manager gets one. As you build trust with Meta it grows, to five, then ten, sometimes higher.

Create a couple of spares as soon as your limit allows. They do not need to spend anything, and they do not need their own payment method. Meta will sometimes warn that an inactive ad account may be closed. In practice that warning has not meant much, and Keala's advice goes further: put a few engagement ads on the spare now so it has a clean approval history before the day you need it.

Create the spare while you are healthy

Once an ad account on your Business Manager is restricted, the option to create another one on that Business Manager is gone for good. The spare has to exist before the problem does.

04

Pixels

You do not need a new pixel for every ad account. One pixel can be shared across several. Business Settings, then Data sources, then Pixels. Pick the pixel, choose Add assets, choose Ad accounts, tick the ones you want, and add.

If you have lost the page that sends install instructions to your funnel builder: Events Manager, open the pixel, scroll to Add events, choose From a new website, then Email instructions.

A shared pixel means your spare ad account inherits everything the main one has learned about your audience the day you switch to it. That alone is a reason to do this before you ever need the spare.

05

If the Business Manager gets disabled

Three lines, in order. Chat with Facebook help if the option appears. Request review through Business Manager. Check Account Quality; if no restriction shows there, create a new Business Manager and run this page's checklist.

The full breakdown by layer, including what to do with a page or an Instagram that is stuck on the dead Business Manager, is on Door 04.
