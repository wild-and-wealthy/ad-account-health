01

What it does, and what it does not do

Marina puts it as two jobs running at once: protect the account, teach the algorithm.

Protect the account. If your score is a ratio of approvals to rejections, the fastest way to fix a bad ratio is to flood the top of it. Keala's version is 25 to 50 simple pieces of content, and in a real cascade up to 100 or 200, each running as an ad at a couple of dollars a day. "You get 2 or 3 rejections. Next time you upload, you got 3 out of 50-something, not 3 out of 12." The endgame is auto-approval. All of his accounts are on it.

Teach the algorithm. Every video that gets watched teaches your pixel and your account who your person actually is. The clearer that picture, the better your lead ads perform later. You also get retargeting pools out of it for free.

Now the honest part. Engagement ads do not un-reject a specific flagged ad. If a video got rejected for the words in it, fifty approved reels will not make that video approvable. The fix for that ad is the language, and the flagged-words box is where that lives. Engagement builds the ratio and warms the audience. It does not launder a bad script.

And organic posts do not count. Only ads go through review, so only ads move the ratio.

02

Our campaign rules

  • Engagement lives in its own campaign. Never inside a LEADS or SALES campaign. Never in the same ad set as a funnel ad.
  • Add new ads inside the existing engagement ad set. Do not duplicate the campaign each time you have new content.
  • Name it so you can filter it. Ours follow MM.YYYY | ENG | Purpose, for example 07.2026 | ENG | Account Health, 07.2026 | ENG | Account Health – Creative Pack.
  • Judge it on approvals and conversations, not CTR or cost per lead. Engagement reach is very cheap, so if you blend it into your funnel numbers it makes your funnel look worse than it really is. Keep the two sets of numbers apart.
If you track in a sheet

Pull spend and clicks at the campaign level and keep only the LEADS and SALES objectives for your funnel rows. Engagement gets its own row, or none at all. Never let it touch your click-through rate.

03

Start today with posts you already have

You do not need to film anything to begin. Marina's first batch was four old Instagram posts at two dollars a day, and three of them approved the same afternoon.

  1. In Ads Manager, create an Engagement campaign. Not the Boost button inside Instagram. Ads Manager, so it sits on your Business Manager ad account.
  2. Set a broad audience: country, age range, gender if it applies. Turn Advantage+ on.
  3. At the ad level, choose Use existing post and pick Instagram as the source.
  4. Choose your most value-driven, least promotional posts. Nothing with a price, nothing with income talk, nothing that mentions the business.

Budget is not the lever here. Account health is driven by the number of ads that get approved, not by how much you spend. One to three dollars a day is genuinely enough.

Still images count too. If you want that spend to do more than bank an approval, put what you would have said in the video into the body text. The cheapest objectives for this work are video views, post engagement, and awareness.

04

Launch settings

This is the setup we use. Keep it in a separate campaign from anything that makes you money.

Objective
Awareness or Engagement. Video views give you watch data; Engagement optimizes for comments.
Budget
$1 to $3 a day per ad set. Health comes from approvals, not spend, so this does not need to run forever. The job is to launch, collect the approvals, and move on. If you want, launch one at $1 a day and leave it toggled on for good.
Placements
All Facebook and Instagram. Let Meta pick.
Creative
A plain video post or photo. No link, no button. The moment you add a destination it stops being pure value in Meta's eyes.
Reading the status
In review or Pending means wait. Active means it cleared. Rejected shows clearly, and Door 03 tells you what to do.
05

Script rules for new value videos

Once the existing or boosted posts are running, you can film new ones that speak to your avatar. Aim for ten script ideas and shoot at least five.

  • Open by naming who it is for, in the describing form: "To the mama who..." Never "Are you a mom who...?" The first describes. The second accuses, and that is a Personal Attributes flag.
  • Never state an age. Use life-stage language.
  • 60 to 90 seconds spoken. Name her pain in a scene she recognizes, give the reframe, hand her one thing she can use today.
  • Teach the thing. Do not gate it. "Comment X and I'll send it to you" turns free value into a lead mechanic, and Meta reads it that way.
  • End with a comment prompt, not a link.
  • Stay in the safe lanes, and pick the ones that fit your avatar. For ours: travel, wellness, slow mornings, working from anywhere, self-care, time, overwhelm, mental load, identity, courage, parenting. Stay completely off the topic of money.

Before you film, check every script for

  • Second-person accusatory phrasing, or any claim about the viewer's situation, body, finances, or health
  • A stated or implied age
  • Income, earnings, or financial-outcome language of any kind
  • Health claims
  • Promised results
  • Any mention of a business, product, offer, price, or opportunity
  • Any lead-capture mechanic: comment to get, DM me, link in bio
  • Anything from our flagged-words list
06

Stack approvals: creatives first

Keala's question to the group: which looks more like a healthy advertiser, twenty creatives approved, or five creatives spread across twenty audiences? New creative beats new audiences. So your first job is more clean videos.

Two of his tricks for getting there faster. First, make the campaign dual-purpose by retargeting anyone who has watched more than a quarter of one of your ads, so the people who have already raised a hand start seeing helpful content from you. Second, the recycling trick: once a video is approved and has run a few days, take it down, re-export it with a small change, such as a couple of seconds added, a different hook, or different music, and upload it again as a new file. New metadata, new approval.

Site position

Creatives first. If you do add extra ad sets for retargeting, build them only around audiences you genuinely want to reach. An ad set you never intend to run is exposure with no benefit.

07

When to test a lead ad again

Keala's target is around a hundred approvals. Get there, then put one real lead ad through and watch whether it clears faster than it used to. That is your signal the account has moved toward auto-approval.

Let the engagement ads keep running a little even after that, so it never reads as bait and switch. And if the lead ad gets rejected anyway, you know where to go: Door 03.
